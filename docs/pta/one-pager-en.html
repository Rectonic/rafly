Rafly brings order to shop inventory, then turns verified surplus into discounted offers.

The problem

In independent shops, stock truth often lives in one person’s head while catalog errors and expiry losses stay hidden. A marketplace cannot repair weak source data, so the shop needs a working control loop first.

Two products, one engine

Control

The shop workspace: catalog import, product identity, recount, expiry watch, exception resolution, and an owner digest.

Market

The buyer surface: human-approved offers only, with price, pickup window, availability, reservation, and pickup code.

7-10 days

How a shop starts

An operator delivers a paid Store Control Sprint: completes a fast full inventory, builds the working catalog, sets recount and expiry routines, trains staff, and launches the shop end to end.

The ask

We are scaling the 50-shop launch cohort. PTA partners can accelerate field delivery with retail expertise and investment support for the next stage of growth.

Accountability by design

  • Cameras and algorithms surface flags and rank risk, then an accountable employee decides.
  • Buyers see an offer only after explicit approval by shop staff.
  • Every recount, publication, and exception retains the actor, time, and full decision trail.

Launching 50 shops

Agreements are in place with 50 shops and launch is under way. The pilot covers stock monitoring, stock analytics, fast full inventory, and turnkey support. Rafly works with any POS or none: the team extracts available data and builds a working catalog.
