# Product Research Questions

Use this file when asking a research-grade model to investigate the product itself rather than the code.

## Marketplace Strategy

1. Which side of the marketplace should LastBite acquire first in Tashkent: buyers, restaurants, bakeries, grocery shops, or hotels/caterers?
2. What minimum seller density is needed for the buyer map/feed to feel useful?
3. Which food categories are most likely to create repeat supply and repeat buyer demand?
4. Should LastBite begin with restaurants/meal packages, grocery expiry inventory, or a narrower niche such as bakeries?
5. How should the app balance urgency and trust so buyers do not feel rushed into unsafe or unclear purchases?

## Buyer Experience

1. What information must be visible before reservation to reduce cancellations and missed pickups?
2. Should pickup code recovery require buyer auth, device possession, SMS/email, or seller confirmation?
3. What are the best cancellation rules for unpaid reservations?
4. What reminder cadence reduces missed pickups without feeling spammy?
5. Should buyers be able to mark a reservation picked up, or should only sellers control collection state?

## Seller Experience

1. What is the minimum seller onboarding checklist for production?
2. Which seller fields are required for trust: address, pickup instructions, phone, operating hours, business registration, photos, payout account?
3. What workflow best fits small sellers: manual packages, inventory-driven offers, POS import, or end-of-day batch creation?
4. What order-management states should sellers see: pending, collected, cancelled, expired, no-show, refunded?
5. What reporting or nudges would motivate sellers to keep publishing?

## Trust, Safety, And Policy

1. What allergen and dietary disclosures should be required versus optional?
2. What food safety and labeling requirements apply in the target market?
3. What should happen when buyers miss pickup windows?
4. What dispute flow is needed if the buyer receives a different package from expected?
5. How should LastBite verify sellers before they can publish?

## Monetization

1. Should the MVP avoid payments and use reservation-only pickup, or introduce in-app payment early?
2. What fee model fits small food sellers: commission, subscription, listing fee, or lead-generation fee?
3. How does payment timing affect cancellation/refund policy and seller trust?
4. Should LastBite subsidize early demand or supply?

## Localization And Market Fit

1. Are English/Russian enough for the first launch, or is Uzbek required for seller operations and buyer trust?
2. Should sellers be required to enter content in multiple languages, or should LastBite provide translation assistance?
3. What cultural norms around food discounts, leftovers, and pickup need product research?

## Analytics To Add Before Public Launch

1. Offer impression to detail conversion.
2. Detail to reservation conversion.
3. Reservation to pickup completion.
4. Cancellation, expiry, and no-show rates.
5. Seller time to first published offer.
6. Repeat buyer and repeat seller rates.
7. Average distance from buyer to reserved offer.
8. Inventory age and waste recovery metrics.
